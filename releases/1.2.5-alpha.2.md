New
- Wireless peripheral battery on the Dynamic Island (stacked on #101), including AJAZZ / AJ179 (dongle VID:PID 249A:5C2F): battery %, online / asleep, and device name (e.g. M041).
- While the mouse is asleep, the last known battery level is kept (never shown as a false 0%).

Notes
- Fork prerelease for testing AJAZZ battery. Based on upstream 1.2.5-alpha.1 plus the #101 device-battery core and AJ179 read-only provider (PR #200 toward MoFeng2223/BrimDeck).
- Charging icon, DPI, and report rate on the island are not in this build yet.
- Windows validation with a plugged-in receiver still needs a local check after install.

新功能
- 灵动岛无线外设电量（叠 #101），含黑爵 / AJ179（接收器 VID:PID 249A:5C2F）：电量 %、在线/休眠、设备名（如 M041）。

- 本仓库预发布，用于试黑爵电量。基于上游 1.2.5-alpha.1，叠加 #101 外设电量核心与 AJ179 只读读取（向上游的 PR #200）。
- 充电图标、DPI、回报率尚未进岛。
- 插上接收器后的 Windows 实测仍建议安装后自行确认。

New
- New battery levels of wireless peripherals.
- New clipboard and file transfer.
- New "Keep above other windows" switch (Settings > General > Window behavior), on by default. When it is off, the notch, the capsule and the indicator can be covered by other windows, and the panel comes to the front when it expands.
- New "Allow window capture" switch (Settings > General > Compatibility), off by default. When it is on, recording software such as OBS can capture the panel on its own with window capture; when it is off, BrimDeck does not appear in the Alt + Tab list.
- "Double-click a resize handle" has a new "Hide handles" option (Settings > Collapsed view > Shortcuts).
- Page shortcuts go from 3 to 10 (Settings > Shortcuts > Switch pages).
- Cursor supports the new three-in-one plans with Grok.

Improvements
- Apps with both a 5-hour and a weekly quota show two concentric quota rings.
- The tracks of progress bars are darker in every theme and easier to see.

Fixes
- Fixed temperatures not being read on some NVMe drives, such as the Kingston KC3000.
- Fixed blank space being left in the app rules in Settings after the list is collapsed or an app is removed.

新功能
- 新增无线外设电量显示功能。
- 新增剪贴板与文件中转功能。
- 新增“显示在其他窗口上方”开关（设置 > 常规 > 窗口行为），默认开启。关闭后胶囊、刘海和指示条可以被其他窗口遮挡，展开时则显示在最前面。
- 新增“允许录制软件捕获窗口”开关（设置 > 常规 > 兼容性），默认关闭。开启后，OBS 等录制软件可以用窗口采集单独捕获面板；关闭时，BrimDeck 不会显示在 Alt + Tab 窗口列表中。
- “双击调整把手”新增“不显示把手”选项（设置 > 收起外观 > 快捷操作）。
- 页面快捷键从 3 个增加到 10 个（设置 > 快捷键 > 切换页面）。
- Cursor 支持 Grok 新版三合一套餐。

改进
- 同时有 5 小时额度和每周额度的应用，配额环显示为同心双环。
- 各主题的进度条底轨加深，更明显。

修复
- 修复金士顿 KC3000 等 NVMe 硬盘读不到温度的问题。
- 修复设置中应用程序规则收起或删除应用后留下空白的问题。

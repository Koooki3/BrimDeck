New
- Apple Music support: the artist and album are shown separately, lyrics are found, and the music visualizer follows Apple Music's sound.
- Full control for Apple Music: after it is enabled, the playback position can be dragged in the panel, shuffle turned on or off, and the repeat mode switched. It works by operating the Apple Music window through UI Automation and lasts until BrimDeck restarts.

Fixes
- Fixed the progress bar of Apple Music jittering.
- Fixed Cursor token details not showing when there were too many records to read within the 20-second limit of one refresh. They are now read one day at a time: days already read are kept, and the remaining days are filled in by later refreshes.

新功能
- 支持 Apple Music：分别显示歌手与专辑，可以查找歌词，音乐律动随 Apple Music 的声音变化。
- 新增 Apple Music 完整控制：启用后可以在面板中拖动播放进度、开关随机播放、切换重复模式。该功能通过界面自动化操作 Apple Music 窗口实现，在 BrimDeck 重启前有效。

修复
- 修复 Apple Music 的进度条抖动的问题。
- 修复 Cursor 使用记录较多、一次刷新在 20 秒时限内读不完时，Token 明细无法显示的问题。现在按天读取：已读完的日期会保留，其余日期在之后的刷新中补全。

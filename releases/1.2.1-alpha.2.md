Improvements
- Reduced ongoing resource use of the notch and capsule by optimizing shadow rendering, scrolling text, quota ring carousels and system reading carousels.
- Reduced memory allocations when refreshing system monitor readings; no sampling is requested for readings when their region has no room to display them.

Fixes
- Fixed the expanded panel unexpectedly collapsing when switching apps within the same window context.
- Improved app rule switching: unchanged rule results no longer trigger a refresh, and changes to the collapsed shape retain their animation.
- Fixed the panel not automatically recovering after losing its topmost status or falling below ordinary windows; other topmost windows covering the panel are only logged to avoid repeatedly competing for the top position.
- Fixed some objects not being released after closing a panel on a secondary monitor.

改进
- 降低刘海和胶囊的常驻开销，优化阴影绘制、滚动文字、配额环及系统读数轮播。
- 减少系统监控读数刷新时的内存分配；读数区域没有显示空间时，不再为其请求采样。

修复
- 修复在相同窗口情境下切换应用时，展开面板意外收起的问题。
- 优化应用程序规则的切换：规则结果相同时不再重复刷新，收起形态发生变化时保留动画过渡。
- 修复面板丢失置顶状态或被普通窗口压到下方后无法自动恢复的问题；其他置顶窗口遮挡面板时仅记录日志，避免反复争抢。
- 修复关闭副显示器面板后，部分对象未能释放的问题。

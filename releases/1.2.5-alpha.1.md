New
- New "Shortcuts" page in Settings.
- New "Mouse pass-through" mode (Settings > General > Compatibility), suited to playing games.
- New "Expand on" setting (Settings > General > Hover and animation): choose "Hover" or "Click".
- Cursor supports the new plans merged with Grok; older plans are shown as before.

Improvements
- The expanded panel now snaps to the top of the screen only when the capsule's "Vertical offset" is 10 DIP or less (it was 20 DIP).

Fixes
- Fixed CPU readings briefly being too high or showing "—" when System monitor is expanded.
- Fixed temperatures also showing "—" when a CPU reading occasionally fails.
- Fixed the CPU and GPU temperatures in the collapsed row first showing "—" after the panel collapses or after the capsule shows a notice.
- Fixed the core columns when collapsed going past their space and the rightmost ones being cut off on CPUs with many cores.
- Fixed the core columns when collapsed occasionally missing a few cores.
- Fixed one side of the page being left being cut off when switching pages (for example from System monitor to Music).

新功能
- 设置新增“快捷键”页。
- 新增“鼠标穿透模式”（设置 > 常规 > 兼容性），适合玩游戏时使用。
- 新增“展开方式”（设置 > 常规 > 悬停与动画），可选择“悬停展开”或“点击展开”。
- Cursor 支持与 Grok 合并后的新套餐，老套餐的显示方式不变。

改进
- 胶囊的“垂直偏移”不超过 10 DIP 时（原为 20 DIP），展开的面板才贴紧屏幕顶端。

修复
- 修复展开系统监控时，CPU 读数短暂偏高或显示“—”的问题。
- 修复 CPU 读数偶尔读取失败时，温度也显示为“—”的问题。
- 修复面板展开后再收起，或胶囊显示通知后，收起行的 CPU 与显卡温度先显示“—”的问题。
- 修复 CPU 核心较多时，收起时的核心柱超出预留宽度、最右侧几根被截掉的问题。
- 修复收起时的核心柱偶尔少几根的问题。
- 修复切换页面时（例如从系统监控切换到音乐），离开的页面一侧被截掉的问题。

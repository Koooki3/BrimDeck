New
- The installer now creates a Start menu shortcut.

Improvements
- Options in the AI quota settings dropdowns now show a description on hover, in both the quota source and the stats app columns.
- The DeepSeek Harness "web app" is now consistently called "local web app".

Fixes
- Fixed too little space at the top of the expanded panel after moving the capsule down.
- Fixed the top-bar buttons sitting at different positions on the usage and music pages, which made them jump when switching pages.
- Fixed the expanded panel not fully covering the capsule when it is moved to the very bottom of a screen without a bottom taskbar.
- Fixed the refresh button's arrow being off-centre.

New

Collapsed and expanded views
- New "Opens" option for the indicator (Settings > Collapsed view > Indicator > Opening): hovering over or clicking the indicator can open only the notch or the capsule.
- New "Part under pointer" option for "Page shown on opening" (Settings > Expanded view > General). For example, with the pointer near the music part of the notch or capsule, the panel opens on the music page; near the system monitor part, it opens on the system monitor page.
- New "Grid layout" for the expanded system monitor page (Settings > System monitor > Display and readings > When expanded > Layout mode). Tiles can be resized freely.

Clipboard and file transfer
- Files in the file transfer can be shared, for now through the Windows share panel and through WeType.
- New "Player volume up" and "Player volume down" music quick actions (Settings > Music > Quick actions). Only the player's volume changes, not the system volume.
- Settings can be exported and imported (Settings > Displays and profiles > Backup).
- New "Click copies only" switch for the clipboard (Settings > Shelf > Clipboard).

System monitor
- New "Memory and drives" option for the system monitor (Settings > System monitor > Display and readings > General), to show either the used or the free amount.
- The system monitor's "Cores per matrix row" can be set (Settings > System monitor > Display and readings > General).

Music
- New "Simulate word highlighting" switch (Settings > Music > Style > General).

AI quota and token statistics
- Quota reading and local token statistics for TRAE, TRAE CN, Qoder (CLI, IDE and desktop app), Qoder CN (CLI, IDE and desktop app), Qianwen Work and QwenWork.
- Quota reading for Doubao (sharing its account with Doubao Work), Dola (the international version), Volcengine Ark, BytePlus ModelArk, Qianwen (China) and QwenCloud.
- Antigravity quotas can be read from Antigravity IDE and the CLI.
- Costs of DeepSeek models are estimated from DeepSeek's official US dollar prices, with peak and off-peak hours told apart by request time.

Device batteries
- Battery levels are supported for Machenike mice such as the M7 Pro, M720, M721, M722, M840, L7 Mix, L7 Air 4K, L8 Pro 2K, L9 X1 and G7.
- Battery levels are supported for the IQUNIX ITX Pro headset and Q101 mouse.
- Battery levels are supported for the EPOS H3PRO Hybrid, GSP 670, GTW 270 Hybrid, H3 Hybrid and GSP 370 wireless headsets.
- Battery levels are supported for the Zhuaudio ZG4 and ZG3 headsets; the GravaStar Mercury mice, S1, S1 Pro, R1, K98 Pro, K1 series and V75 Ultra; the VGN V98Pro V3, V98Pro V4, V87Pro V2 and V108Pro wired keyboards; and the VXE V87pro.
- Battery levels are supported for the LANGTU T98 RGB and T75, the AJAZZ AK820 Max Plus, Rapoo office receivers (such as the 1870), and LANGTU keyboards connected by cable such as the T87, MC68 and BOOM68.
- AirPods 4, AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation and AirPods Max (USB-C) are recognized, as are the Beats Fit Pro, Solo Pro, Studio Buds, Studio Buds+ and Studio Pro.

Improvements
- KuGou lyrics are read from the lyrics KuGou saves on this PC.
- Switching in the stacked shelf layout is changed (Settings > Shelf > Page layout > Switching): "Switch when the pointer hovers over the side strip" is now "Switch by turning the wheel over the side strip", and "Switch with a two-finger sideways swipe or Shift + wheel" is now "Switch with a two-finger sideways swipe" and works more smoothly.
- Lyric search is improved, so lyrics are found more often.
- When device batteries are read again, the last readings are kept and shown first.
- Battery levels of the WOBKEY Rainy75 V2 and Rainy98 are read more reliably through the receiver.
- Some settings look better, and some defaults are improved.
- Some pages look better.

Fixes
- Resizing the token statistics page lags less when it holds a lot of content.
- The music page flickers less.
- Fixed a device that could be read being taken as unreadable in some cases and never read again.
- Drop-down lists in settings scroll by pixels, so no blank space is left at the bottom when there are more options than fit.
- Fixed the maximized settings window being covered by the taskbar, so the last items at the bottom and in the left list could not be clicked.
- Fixed files not being dropped onto the panel, and context menus not responding to hover and clicks, while "Force on top" is on.
- Fixed the shadow around the collapsed capsule or notch blocking clicks on the windows below it.
- Fixed some dialogs flickering continuously at 125% and 225% scale.
- Fixed clicking the gap between the indicator and the screen edge not opening the panel when the panel opens on click.
- Fixed "Enable full control" showing briefly when seeking or changing songs while NetEase Cloud Music is under full control.
- Fixed the expanded panel closing after clicking a category in the clipboard and file transfer while "Allow window capture" is on.
- Fixed the page switch, clock and settings button at the top of the expanded panel moving when switching pages.
- Fixed the area for resizing the panel being in the wrong place after switching between pages of the same size.
- Fixed a beta update still being shown after "Receive beta updates" was turned off during an update check.
- Fixed battery levels not being read from BETOP KP20 and some KP40 controllers.
- Fixed the MCHOSE V9 PRO headset always showing "No battery reported". ATK N9 V2 series, FIFINE, XIBERIA, AULA A9, some Zhuaudio headsets and some other MCHOSE headsets are now read the same way.
- A wired device shared by LANGTU, LEOBOG and VXE is no longer always shown as VXE V87pro, and shows the name the device reports instead.
- Some NuPhy receivers no longer show wrong model names such as Air75 V2 and Halo75 V2.

新功能

收起与展开
- 指示条新增“展开为”选项（设置 > 收起外观 > 指示条 > 展开），悬停或点击指示条，可以只展开为刘海或胶囊。
- “每次打开时显示”新增“鼠标所在的区段”选项（设置 > 展开外观 > 通用）。例如鼠标靠近刘海或胶囊的音乐区域，则展开为音乐页面；鼠标靠近系统监控区域，则展开为系统监控页面。
- 系统监控展开页新增“网格布局”（设置 > 系统监控 > 显示与读数 > 展开时 > 排版方式）。可自由改变区块大小。

剪贴板与文件中转
- 文件中转新增分享功能。目前支持 Windows 自带的分享与微信输入法的传输文件。
- 音乐快捷操作新增“增大播放器音量”“减小播放器音量”（设置 > 音乐 > 快捷操作）。只调节该播放器音量，而不是系统整体音量。
- 新增配置导出与导入（设置 > 显示器与配置 > 备份）。
- 剪贴板新增“单击只复制，不粘贴”开关（设置 > 中转站 > 剪贴板）。

系统监控
- 系统监控新增“内存与硬盘”选项（设置 > 系统监控 > 显示与读数 > 通用），可以选择显示已用量或剩余量。
- 系统监控可以设置“点阵每行核心数”（设置 > 系统监控 > 显示与读数 > 通用）。

音乐
- 新增“模拟逐字高亮”开关（设置 > 音乐 > 样式 > 通用）。

AI 配额与 Token 统计
- 新增 TRAE、TRAE CN、Qoder（CLI+IDE+桌面版）、Qoder CN（CLI+IDE+桌面版）、千问办公、QwenWork 的配额读取与本地 Token 统计。
- 新增豆包（与豆包工作共用账号）、海外版 Dola、火山方舟、BytePlus ModelArk、千问（国内）、QwenCloud 配额读取。
- Antigravity 支持读取 Antigravity IDE 与 CLI 的额度。
- DeepSeek 模型按 DeepSeek 官方美元单价估算费用，并按请求时间区分高峰与低峰时段。

外设电量
- 新增支持机械师 M7 Pro、M720、M721、M722、M840、L7 Mix、L7 Air 4K、L8 Pro 2K、L9 X1、G7 等鼠标。
- 新增支持 IQUNIX ITX Pro 耳机与 Q101 鼠标。
- 新增支持 EPOS H3PRO Hybrid、GSP 670、GTW 270 Hybrid、H3 Hybrid、GSP 370 无线耳机。
- 新增支持竹林鸟烛龙 ZG4、朱雀 ZG3 耳机，重力星球 Mercury 鼠标、S1、S1 Pro、R1、K98 Pro、K1 系列、V75 Ultra，VGN V98Pro V3、V98Pro V4、V87Pro V2、V108Pro 有线键盘，以及 VXE V87pro。
- 新增支持狼途 T98 RGB、T75，AJAZZ AK820 Max Plus，雷柏办公系列接收器（如 1870），以及狼途 T87、MC68、BOOM68 等用数据线连接的键盘。
- AirPods 新增识别 AirPods 4、AirPods 4 降噪款、USB-C 版 AirPods Max；Beats 新增识别 Fit Pro、Solo Pro、Studio Buds、Studio Buds+、Studio Pro。

改进
- 酷狗音乐歌词会读取保存在本机的歌词。
- 中转站叠放时的切换方式调整（设置 > 中转站 > 页面布局 > 切换方式），“指针悬停在侧条时切换”改为“在侧条上滚动滚轮切换”，“触控板双指横滑或 Shift + 滚轮切换”改为“触控板双指横滑切换”并优化体验。
- 歌词查找优化，减少查询不到歌词的情况。
- 重新读取设备电量时，增加上一次读数缓存。
- WOBKEY Rainy75 V2、Rainy98 通过接收器读取电量更稳定。
- 部分设置项展示效果优化、默认值优化。
- 部分页面显示效果优化。

修复
- Token 统计页内容太多时，拖动调整大小时减少卡顿。
- 减少音乐页面的闪烁情况。
- 修复部分特殊情况下设备明明可以读取但是被判定为读取失败从而永远不读取的情况。
- 设置中的下拉框改为按像素滚动，选项多于一屏时底部不再留空白。
- 修复设置窗口最大化后被任务栏遮住，底部和左侧最后几项无法点击的问题。
- 修复开启“强制置顶”后，文件无法拖进面板、右键菜单无法悬停和点击的问题。
- 修复收起时胶囊、刘海周围的阴影挡住下方窗口点击的问题。
- 修复部分弹窗在 125%、225% 缩放下不停闪烁的问题。
- 修复点击展开时，点击指示条与屏幕边缘之间的空隙无法展开的问题。
- 修复网易云音乐开启完整控制后，跳转或切歌时短暂显示“启用完整控制”的问题。
- 修复开启“允许录制软件捕获窗口”时，点击剪贴板和文件中转的分类后，展开面板会关闭的问题。
- 修复切换页面时，展开面板顶部的页面切换按钮、时钟和设置按钮移位的问题。
- 修复在大小相同的页面之间切换时，调整面板大小的拖动区域位置不对的问题。
- 修复检查更新期间关闭“接收测试版更新”后，仍然显示测试版更新的问题。
- 修复北通鲲鹏20 和部分鲲鹏40 手柄读不到电量的问题。
- 修复迈从 V9 PRO 耳机一直显示“未报告电量”的问题。ATK N9 V2 系列、FIFINE、西伯利亚、AULA A9、竹林鸟和迈从的部分耳机也改用同样的方式读取。
- 狼途、LEOBOG、VXE 共用的一款有线设备不再一律显示为 VXE V87pro，改为显示设备自己报告的名称。
- NuPhy 部分接收器不再显示 Air75 V2、Halo75 V2 等错误的型号名称。

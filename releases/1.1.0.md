New
- The notch, capsule and indicator bar now support custom size and position.
- Added global hotkeys to hide or show the notch and to expand or collapse the panel.
- Added "Hide panel / Show panel" to the tray menu.
- Added multi-monitor support.
- In NetEase Cloud Music full control mode, the music page now has a "Like" button.

Improvements
- The music page now uses a top-and-bottom layout: cover, title, artist and lyrics on top, with a full-width progress bar and playback controls below. All elements scale smoothly with the panel size.
- Adjusted the "Standard" size of the music page.
- Refined the layout of the expanded panel's top bar for a cleaner look.
- Tooltips in the Token usage details table now show model unit prices and exact token counts.
- "7 天" at the bottom of the usage panel is now "七日" in Chinese and "Week" in English.

Fixes
- Fixed the panel getting stuck after switching the primary display when monitors use different scaling.
- Fixed the settings window changing width when its scroll bar appears or disappears.
- Info icons in settings are now aligned with their title text.

新功能
- 刘海、胶囊、指示条支持自定义尺寸与位置。
- 新增全局快捷键隐藏或显示刘海、展开或收起面板。
- 托盘菜单新增“隐藏面板/显示面板”。
- 支持多显示器。
- 网易云音乐完整控制模式下，音乐页新增"收藏"按钮。

改进
- 音乐页改为上下布局：上方为封面、歌名、歌手和歌词，下方为通栏进度条与播放控制，各元素随面板尺寸连续缩放。
- 音乐页“标准”尺寸调整。
- 展开面板顶栏排布细微调整，看起来更美观。
- Token 用量明细表的悬浮提示显示模型单价和精确token数值。
- 用量面板底部的“7 天”改为“七日”，英文改为“Week”。

修复
- 修复多显示器缩放尺寸不一致时切换主显示器后面板卡住的问题。
- 修复设置页出现或取消滚动条时窗口宽度变化的问题。
- 设置中的说明图标与标题文字对齐。

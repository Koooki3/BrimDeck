New
- Settings can be exported and imported (Settings > Displays and profiles > Backup).
- AI quota and token statistics support Qoder and Qoder CN, read from the Qoder CLI, the desktop app and the Qoder IDE.
- AI quota supports Qianwen (China) and QwenCloud.
- AI quota and token statistics support QwenWork CN and QwenWork.
- Battery levels are supported for the LANGTU T98 RGB and T75, the AJAZZ AK820 Max Plus, Rapoo office receivers (such as the 1870), and LANGTU keyboards connected by cable such as the T87, MC68 and BOOM68. Except for the Rapoo 1870, none are verified with real devices.

Improvements
- The system monitor's "Cores per matrix row" can be set (Settings > System monitor > Display and readings > General).
- When collapsed, quota rings in the multi-ring style scale with the panel height and are better sized.
- After PawnIO is installed or uninstalled, the settings page updates its status on its own.
- LEOBOG Hi75, A75, A98, Hi8, Hi86, Hi98 and other models, and the LANGTU L18, show their model names when connected through the receiver.

Fixes
- Fixed clicking the open panel closing the clipboard and file transfer while "Allow window capture" is on.
- Fixed the page switch, clock and settings button at the top of the open panel moving when switching pages.
- Fixed the area for resizing the panel being in the wrong place after switching between pages of the same size.
- Fixed a beta update still being shown after "Receive beta updates" was turned off during an update check. The newest stable release is offered instead.
- Fixed battery levels not being read from BETOP KP20 and some KP40 controllers (not verified with real devices).
- A wired device shared by LANGTU, LEOBOG and VXE is no longer always shown as VXE V87pro, and shows the name the device reports instead.
- Some NuPhy receivers no longer show model names such as Air75 V2 and Halo75 V2.
- Fixed the MCHOSE V9 PRO headset always showing "No battery reported". ATK N9 V2 series, FIFINE, XIBERIA, AULA A9, some Zhuaudio headsets and some other MCHOSE headsets are now read the same way. Except for the MCHOSE V9 PRO, none are verified with real devices.

新功能
- 新增配置导出与导入（设置 > 显示器与配置 > 备份）。
- AI 配额与token统计新增 Qoder 与 Qoder CN，支持 Qoder CLI、桌面版、Qoder IDE。
- AI 配额新增千问（国内）与 QwenCloud。
- AI 配额与token统计新增千问办公与 QwenWork。
- 外设电量新增支持狼途 T98 RGB、T75，AJAZZ AK820 Max Plus，雷柏办公系列接收器（如 1870），以及狼途 T87、MC68、BOOM68 等用数据线连接的键盘。除雷柏 1870 外，未在实际设备上验证。

改进
- 系统监控可以自定义“点阵每行核心数”（设置 > 系统监控 > 显示与读数 > 通用）。
- 收起时，多环样式的配额环大小随面板高度变化，大小优化。
- 安装或卸载 PawnIO 后，设置页会自动更新状态。
- LEOBOG Hi75、A75、A98、Hi8、Hi86、Hi98 等型号和狼途 L18 通过接收器连接时，会显示型号名称。

修复
- 修复开启“允许录制软件捕获窗口”后，点击展开面板会关闭剪贴板和文件中转站的问题。
- 修复切换页面时，展开面板顶部的页面切换按钮、时钟和设置按钮会移位的问题。
- 修复在大小相同的页面之间切换时，调整面板大小的拖动区域位置不对的问题。
- 修复检查更新的过程中关闭“接收测试版更新”后，仍然显示测试版更新的问题。现在会改为提供最新的正式版。
- 修复北通鲲鹏20 和部分鲲鹏40 手柄读不到电量的问题（未在实际设备上验证）。
- 狼途、LEOBOG、VXE 共用的一款有线设备不再一律显示为 VXE V87pro，改为显示设备自己报告的名称。
- NuPhy 部分接收器不再显示 Air75 V2、Halo75 V2 等型号名称。
- 修复迈从 V9 PRO 耳机一直显示“未报告电量”的问题。ATK N9 V2 系列、FIFINE、西伯利亚、AULA A9、竹林鸟部分耳机，以及迈从的部分其他耳机，也改用同样的方式读取电量。除迈从 V9 PRO 外，未在实际设备上验证过。

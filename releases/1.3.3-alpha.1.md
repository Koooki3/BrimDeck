New
- New "Click copies only" switch for the clipboard (Settings > Shelf > Clipboard), off by default.
- New "Memory and drives" option for the system monitor (Settings > System monitor > Display and readings > General), to show either the used or the free amount.
- Battery levels are supported for the Zhuaudio ZG4 and ZG3 headsets; the GravaStar Mercury mice, S1, S1 Pro, R1, K98 Pro, K1 series and V75 Ultra; the VGN V98Pro V3, V98Pro V4, V87Pro V2 and V108Pro wired keyboards; and the VXE V87pro (not verified with real devices).
- Costs of DeepSeek models are estimated from DeepSeek's official US dollar prices, with peak and off-peak hours told apart by request time.

Improvements
- Device battery levels show how they were read: "Stepped level" for levels read in steps, and "From voltage" for levels worked out from the voltage, instead of "Estimated" for both.
- Battery levels of the WOBKEY Rainy75 V2 and Rainy98 are read more reliably through the receiver.
- Text in the settings window has more even stroke weight and spacing at 150% scale and above, and button text is centered vertically in the button.

新功能
- 剪贴板新增“单击只复制，不粘贴”开关（设置 > 中转站 > 剪贴板），默认关闭。
- 系统监控新增“内存与硬盘”选项（设置 > 系统监控 > 显示与读数 > 通用），可选择显示已用或剩余的量。
- 外设电量新增支持竹林鸟烛龙 ZG4、朱雀 ZG3 耳机，重力星球 Mercury 鼠标、S1、S1 Pro、R1、K98 Pro、K1 系列、V75 Ultra，VGN V98Pro V3、V98Pro V4、V87Pro V2、V108Pro 有线键盘，以及 VXE V87pro（未通过实际设备验证）。
- DeepSeek 模型按 DeepSeek 官方美元单价估算费用，按请求时间区分高峰与低峰时段。

改进
- 设备电量注明读数方式：按电量档位读取的显示“按电量档位”，按电压推算的显示“按电压换算”，不再统一显示“估算”。
- WOBKEY Rainy75 V2、Rainy98 通过接收器读取电量更稳定。
- 设置窗口的文字在 150% 及以上缩放时粗细和间距更均匀，按钮文字在按钮内上下居中。

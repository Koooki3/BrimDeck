New
- OpenCode as a quota and statistics source. It uses the credentials that OpenCode (CLI or desktop app) signed in to OpenCode Console with, shows the OpenCode Go quotas of the signed-in workspace (5-hour, weekly and monthly), and counts the tokens and cost of OpenCode on this computer.
- OpenCode Go source: enter an OpenCode Console API key to read the quotas.
- Disk usage in System monitor: one row for each internal drive with used and total space. When a drive has several partitions, its bar is split by partition. USB drives, SD cards, network drives and optical drives are not listed.
- Temperatures in System monitor: GPU, solid-state drives, and the CPU when the computer's firmware provides it. No administrator rights or drivers are needed, and readings that are not available are not shown.
- The collapsed row can show disk usage and temperatures: for CPU, GPU and disk, choose usage, temperature or both; for disk, choose one or more drive letters.
- New built-in theme "Island Holiday": light by day and dark at night, switching gradually by local time. The daytime hours can be changed in Settings.
- New built-in theme "Pixel Blocks": a stepped outline, bevelled border and block texture; corners inside the panel become square with this theme.
- New "Pixel" clock style, available with any theme.
- Custom themes can define a night appearance (night, daylight) and pixel effects (pixel, texture). See the theme documentation.
- Apple Music desktop app: lyrics from Apple Music itself are shown first, read from its local cache. When there are none, synced lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB, QQ Music and NetEase Cloud Music in that order.
- A portable ZIP package is now published, and the portable version supports in-app updates.

Improvements
- Panel animations: collapsing is faster than expanding (270 ms by default); expanding or collapsing with a shortcut or Esc is shorter and no longer overshoots; moving the pointer away while the panel is expanding now collapses it at once without a pause.
- Page switching is quicker. The panel's change of shape and the new page's entrance take less time and no longer follow "Expand and collapse animation duration"; when pages are switched quickly, the previous page keeps fading out instead of disappearing.
- Switching themes now fades from one to the other.
- The date range picker under "By time" on the AI usage page is animated: the selection slides between dates, the range fades in and out, and months slide when changed. Today is marked with a dot.
- Settings drop-down lists are limited to about eight rows and can be scrolled with the mouse wheel. Reordering on the Apps page no longer follows the panel animation duration.
- System monitor: resizing the panel switches layouts more smoothly; with the process list on the right, the bottoms of both columns line up; at small sizes the memory row also shows the commit charge; with the GPU as the main gauge, its engine columns fill the graph.
- Token costs are now estimated with the price of each model maker's own provider on OpenRouter; models without one keep the previous catalog price.
- Apple Music: playback position is more accurate; lyric lines switch 0.2 seconds earlier; "♪" is shown only for gaps of 5 seconds or more.
- Lower resource use:
  - While the display is off (but not locked), scrolling titles, the equalizer and its audio capture, quota ring carousels and reading sampling are paused.
  - While music plays, the collapsed title scrolls three times and then stops at the start; it scrolls again when the song or lyric line changes or the pointer moves onto the island.
  - The equalizer redraws only when its bars change, and audio analysis runs less often.
  - Drop-down lists no longer capture their image continuously while open.

Fixes
- Fixed lyrics lookup through QQ Music no longer working, because its previous search address returns an error.
- Fixed the panel expanding when another window is dragged past the top of the screen. After releasing the window, move the pointer away and back to expand the panel.
- Fixed a tooltip staying on the new page after switching pages with the mouse wheel, or after the panel switched to the music page when playback started.
- Fixed the expanded panel shifting left and right between pages after the capsule became wider while music played.
- Fixed the panel briefly showing its collapsed shape before hiding again, when it was opened with the expand shortcut while hidden and then collapsed.
- Fixed DeepSeek Harness sessions with very long lines or very large files using a lot of memory, or making the whole source fail to load.

新功能
- 新增 OpenCode 额度与统计来源。使用 OpenCode（命令行或桌面版）登录 OpenCode Console 的凭据，显示所登录工作区的 OpenCode Go 额度（5 小时、每周、每月），并统计本机 OpenCode 的 Token 用量与费用。
- 新增 OpenCode Go 来源，填写 OpenCode Console 的 API 密钥即可读取额度。
- 系统监控新增硬盘占用：每块内置硬盘一行，显示已用空间与总容量；一块硬盘有多个分区时，占用条按分区分段。U 盘、SD 卡、网络驱动器和光驱不列出。
- 系统监控新增温度：显示 GPU、固态硬盘的温度，电脑固件提供时还显示 CPU 温度。不需要管理员权限，不安装驱动；读取不到的项不显示。
- 收起行可显示硬盘占用与温度：CPU、GPU 和硬盘可分别选择显示占用、温度或两者；硬盘可选择一个或多个盘符。
- 新增内置主题“海岛假日”：白天为浅色外观，夜间为深色外观，按本地时间渐变切换。白天时段可在设置中修改。
- 新增内置主题“像素方块”：阶梯形轮廓、斜面描边和方块纹理，面板内的圆角随主题变为直角。
- 新增“像素”时间样式，任何主题下均可选用。
- 自定义主题支持日夜外观（night、daylight）和像素效果（pixel、texture），写法见主题文档。
- Apple Music 桌面版优先显示 Apple Music 自己的歌词（读取本机缓存）；没有时依次从 LRCLIB、QQ 音乐、网易云查找同步歌词。
- 提供免安装压缩包，免安装版支持应用内更新。

改进
- 面板动画调整：收起比展开更快（默认 270 毫秒）；使用快捷键或 Esc 展开、收起时动画更短，展开不再回弹；展开途中移开鼠标时立即收回，不再停顿。
- 切换页面更快：面板外形变化和新页面进入的时间缩短，且不再随“展开与收起动画时长”变化；快速连续切换时，上一页继续淡出，不会突然消失。
- 切换主题时渐变过渡。
- AI 用量页“按时间”的日期范围选择器加入动画：选中标记在日期间滑动，区间底色淡入淡出，切换月份时滑动；当天日期下方显示圆点。
- 设置中的下拉列表最多显示约八行，可用鼠标滚轮滚动；“应用”页调整顺序的动画不再随面板动画时长变化。
- 系统监控：调整面板大小时版面切换更平滑；进程列表在右侧时，左右两栏的下沿对齐；小尺寸下内存行也显示提交量；以 GPU 为主仪表时，引擎柱填满图表宽度。
- Token 费用估算改按 OpenRouter 上模型厂商自己渠道的价格计算；厂商没有自己渠道的模型仍使用原来的目录价格。
- Apple Music：播放进度更准确；歌词提前 0.2 秒切换；句子之间的空档达到 5 秒才显示“♪”。
- 降低资源占用：
  - 显示器关闭（未锁屏）时，暂停滚动标题、均衡器及其音频采集、配额环轮播和读数采样。
  - 播放音乐时，收起行的标题滚动三遍后停在开头；换歌、歌词换行或鼠标移到岛上时重新滚动。
  - 均衡器只在柱子变化时重绘，音频分析频率降低。
  - 下拉列表打开期间不再持续截图。

修复
- 修复通过 QQ 音乐查找歌词失效的问题（原搜索地址已返回错误）。
- 修复拖动其他窗口经过屏幕顶部时面板被展开的问题。松开窗口后，需将鼠标移出再移入才会展开。
- 修复用鼠标滚轮切换页面，或开始播放后自动切换到音乐页时，上一页的提示框留在新页面上的问题。
- 修复播放音乐使胶囊变宽后，切换页面时展开面板左右错位的问题。
- 修复隐藏状态下用展开快捷键打开面板、再收起时，先短暂显示收起形态才重新隐藏的问题。
- 修复 DeepSeek Harness 会话中出现超长行或超大文件时，占用大量内存或整个来源显示读取失败的问题。

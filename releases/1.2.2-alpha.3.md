New
- OpenRouter as a quota source.
- Off-peak hours for quota sources: during off-peak hours, quotas are shown in the off-peak color.
- Support for multiple GPUs: choose which GPU System monitor shows.
- The panel can be resized with a shortcut.
- The order of the expanded panel's pages can be changed by dragging them in Settings.
- The collapsed row can show the CPU, GPU and disk usage and temperature side by side on one line.
- New Apple Music player setting: "Always enable full control".
- New NetEase Cloud Music player setting: "Enable full control when it starts".
- New built-in theme "Island Holiday 2": by day, a lagoon under a clear sky with glints on the water, palm leaf shadows and a rounded clock; at night, the sea under moonlight. The original "Island Holiday" is unchanged.
- Windows high contrast: when it is on, the panel, the tray menu and the clock use the system colors, without gradients, shadows, textures or glow. Turning high contrast off restores the previous appearance.

Improvements
- The disk section of System monitor shows one item per drive letter. When there is enough width, the items are shown in two columns of up to three rows, and the letters that do not fit are combined into "+N".
- Collapsed row layout: carousels stop only on whole items, and readings are no longer squeezed.
- Improved the scrolling of the collapsed row's title.
- Improved the scroll bars in the expanded panel.

Fixes
- Fixed the previous panel briefly appearing when the panel was expanded with the shortcut while hidden.

新功能
- 新增 OpenRouter 配额来源。
- 配额来源新增“低峰时间”：低峰时段内，额度改用低峰颜色显示。
- 支持多块显卡：系统监控可选择显示哪一块显卡。
- 可通过快捷键调整面板大小。
- 展开面板的页面可在设置中拖动调整顺序。
- 收起行的 CPU、GPU 和硬盘读数新增“同一行”排列，占用和温度可以并排显示。
- 播放器设置新增 Apple Music“始终启用完整控制”开关。
- 播放器设置新增网易云音乐“启动后自动启用完整控制”开关。
- 新增内置主题“海岛假日 2”：白天是晴空下的泻湖海面，有水光和棕榈叶影，时钟使用圆润字体；夜间是月光下的海面。原有的“海岛假日”保持不变。
- 适配 Windows 高对比度：开启后，面板、托盘菜单和时钟都改用系统颜色，不显示渐变、阴影、纹理和辉光。关闭高对比度后恢复原来的外观。

改进
- 系统监控页的磁盘区改为每个盘符单独一项。宽度足够时分两列显示，最多三行，多出的盘符合并为“+N”。
- 收起行排布调整：轮播只停在完整的一项上，读数不再被压缩。
- 优化收起行标题的滚动方式。
- 优化展开面板的滚动条。

修复
- 修复隐藏状态下用快捷键展开面板时，先闪出上次面板画面的问题。
